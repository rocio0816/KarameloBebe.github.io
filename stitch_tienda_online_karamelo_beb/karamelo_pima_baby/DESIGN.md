## Brand & Style

This design system expresses pure tenderness, maternal warmth, and the organic, cloud-soft touch of premium Peruvian Pima cotton. Designed for discerning parents, gift-givers, and modern families, it establishes a sanctuary of calm through generous negative space, pillow-like rounded surfaces, and comforting pastel hues. 

The aesthetic is a delicate balance of **Warm Minimalism** and **Tactile Softness**:
- **Purity & Softness**: Surfaces feel airy and weightless, mirroring natural cotton clouds with zero harsh edges or aggressive contrasts.
- **Emotional Calm**: Interface interactions offer fluid, peaceful feedback to avoid overstimulation for sleep-deprived new parents.
- **Crafted Elegance**: Photography and typography take center stage, allowing fabric texture, natural drape, and artisanal detailing to communicate unmatched heirloom quality.

## Colors

The color palette captures the soothing aura of a nursery filled with natural sunlight and sweet dreams. The interface defaults strictly to light mode to maintain transparency, hygiene, and brightness.

- **Primary (`#F472B6`)**: A delicate, sweet rose used for primary actions, highlight states, and focal purchase triggers.
- **Secondary (`#38BDF8`)**: A dreamy sky blue signifying tranquility, breathable comfort, and gentle balance across unisex and baby boy collections.
- **Tertiary (`#FDA4AF`)**: Soft peach-blossom tone utilized for micro-badges, fabric certifications (e.g., 100% Pima Organic), and subtle celebratory accents.
- **Neutral (`#334155`)**: A soft slate-warm gray engineered to replace harsh pure blacks, offering gentle yet WCAG AAA contrast for body typography and quiet iconography.
- **Canvas & Backdrops**: Layered variations of pure white (`#FFFFFF`) grounded by subtle cloud tones (`#F8FAFC`, `#FCE7F3` at 40% tint, and `#E0F2FE` at 30% tint) for breathable section zoning.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy marries the organic geometry of **Quicksand** for headlines with the warm, open legibility of **Nunito Sans** for body and metadata.

- **Headlines & Editorial**: Quicksand’s rounded terminals evoke natural softness, playfulness, and approachability without feeling childish or chaotic.
- **Body & Functional UI**: Nunito Sans provides high x-height and clear distinction between similar glyphs, ensuring product descriptions, care instructions, and sizing tables remain instantly readable on mobile devices.
- **Letter Spacing & Line Height**: Generous line heights are enforced systematically to provide visual air and calm across long textile descriptions and reviews.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid, breathable 12-column grid layout provides spaciousness and effortless vertical rhythm across desktop displays, consolidating gracefully into 4 columns on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop Layout**: Fluid container with a max-width of `1280px` centered, utilizing `3rem` section margins and `1.5rem` gutters to permit product imagery to breathe.
- **Mobile Experience**: Optimized for thumb navigation; compact `1.25rem` outer canvas margin ensures maximized touch target surface and clear visual focus for small screens.
- **Rhythm**: Element padding leans on `space-md` (`1rem`) and `space-lg` (`1.5rem`), strictly avoiding dense data clustering. Sections flow with cloud-like expansiveness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy abandons clinical dark drop shadows in favor of **Soft Cloud Shadows** and pastel ambient glow layers.

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Pure background surfaces (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`) with borderless boundaries.
- **Level 1 (Pillow Resting)**: Soft cards and product tiles use extra-diffuse tinted shadows: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating & Hover)**: Interactive items lift effortlessly upon hover or touch: `0px 16px 32px -6px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.12), 0px 6px 16px -4px rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Drawers)**: Cart flyouts, size guides, and modals adopt a translucent frosted glass backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with pure white surfaces elevated by `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(51, 65, 85, 0.10)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-shaped, ultra-friendly curvature level (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Pill Philosophy**: Buttons, tags, chips, and notification capsules feature fully rounded pill geometries (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to elicit a sensation of soft pebbles and plush baby garments.
- **Cards & Containers**: Large containers utilize generous radii (`rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` / `1.5rem` to `2rem`), entirely eliminating sharp corners.
- **Imagery**: Product cutouts and lifestyle hero images adopt smooth, pill-curved frames or organic pebble masks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), solid `#F472B6` background, crisp white typography (`label-lg`), with subtle pink ambient glow shadow. Gentle scale-up transform on hover (`scale-[1.02]`).
- **Secondary**: Pill shape, translucent sky wash background (`#E0F2FE`), text color `#0284C7`, hover transition to `#BAE6FD`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: No outline; neutral slate text with a pastel hover bloom.

### Product Cards
- Built with seamless `rounded-2xl` corners, a pure white container background, and ultra-soft Level 1 shadow.
- Imagery incorporates a subtle pale-tint background (`#FDF2F8` or `#F0F9FF`) to make white cotton clothes pop naturally.
- Direct "Quick Add" trigger appears as a rounded floating pill icon in the bottom corner of the image.

### Chips & Badges
- Used for fabric properties (e.g., "100% Algodón Pima", "Hipoalergénico", "0-3 Meses").
- Enclosed in compact pill structures (`rounded-full`) with light pastel fills (`#FCE7F3`, `#E0F2FE`) and corresponding saturated text.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Text fields use `rounded-2xl` borders, a soft slate-tinted background (`#F8FAFC`), and subtle border transitions into `#F472B6` on focus with zero harsh outlines.
- Age/Size selectors appear as soft circular or rounded-pill toggle chips with pillowy active states.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Circular form factors for both radios and checkboxes to keep the soft, baby-safe visual metaphor intact.
- Active states glow with a gentle `#F472B6` fill and a centered white checkmark or dot.

### Special E-Commerce Elements
- **Gift Box Wrapper Toggle**: Whimsical illustrated badge with a soft blush card and smooth toggle switch.
- **Care & Fabric Accordion**: Borderless collapsible tabs with rounded container headers and generous internal padding.
